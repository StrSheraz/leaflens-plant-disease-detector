What is Grad-CAM?

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping highlights which parts of the image the AI used to make its decision. Red regions show where the model focused most — typically where disease symptoms are visible.

Accuracy Note
Heatmap Limitations

The highlighted region shows the overall area the model considered important. This may include leaf edges, texture patterns, or color contrast — reflecting training patterns, not necessarily exact symptom locations.
